**Paush Purnima 2027 is on Friday, 22 January.** The Purnima tithi runs from 9:31 pm on Thursday, 21 January, to 5:47 pm on Friday (IST). That last minute is the exact full moon, and it's the closest full moon of 2027.

It's a busy day. Kalpvas begins at the Magh Mela in Prayagraj, and it's Shakambhari Purnima too. And in New Delhi, the moon comes up at 5:46 pm, one minute before it's full.

## Paush Purnima 2027 date and time

| | Date and time (IST) |
| --- | --- |
| Purnima begins | Thursday, 21 January, 9:31 pm |
| Purnima ends (the exact full moon) | Friday, 22 January, 5:47 pm |
| Purnima fast (vrat) and snan | Friday, 22 January |
| Moonrise, New Delhi | Friday, 22 January, 5:46 pm |

Purnima begins late on Thursday night, so Friday has it at sunrise and through the day. That makes Friday the day for the fast and the holy dip.

The tithi times are the same everywhere in India. They come from Tithi Vidya, which works out the sun and moon from NASA JPL's [DE440 data](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/doc/de440_de441.html) ([here's how](/how-it-works)). Our calculator doesn't do moonrise yet, so moonrise comes from a separate script on the same data.

## The closest full moon of 2027

I worked out how far the moon is at each of the 12 full moons of 2027. This one wins: 357,644 km from Earth at 5:47 pm IST. The farthest, in July, is 405,979 km away. A full moon this close is what [NASA calls a supermoon](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/supermoons/), so look up on Friday evening.

The closeness also explains something odd. This Purnima lasts only 20 hours 17 minutes, the shortest of the year, because the moon moves fastest when it's near Earth. The same thing happened with [Dattatreya Jayanti's full moon](/blog/dattatreya-jayanti) in December 2026, the closest of that year.

The moon rises at different times across India, while Purnima ends at one moment for everyone. So in the east the moon comes up with Purnima still running, and in the west just after it ends.

## Kalpvas begins at the Sangam

Paush Purnima is the day [kalpvas begins](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2026/01/03/lakhs-bathe-in-freezing-sangam-waters-as-magh-mela-begins-in-prayagraj.amp.html): the month some pilgrims spend living by the river at the Magh Mela, bathing twice a day and eating one meal. In 2027 it runs to Maghi Purnima, Saturday, 20 February.

This year the mela itself opens a week earlier, on Makar Sankranti, 15 January. Our [Magh Mela 2027 post](/blog/magh-mela) has all six bathing days and why the order flipped this year. At Prayagraj the sun rises at 6:49 am on Friday. Purnima runs until 5:47 pm, so a dip at any time of the day falls inside it.

## Shakambhari Purnima

Paush Purnima is also [Shakambhari Purnima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shakambhari_Purnima), the last day of the eight-day Shakambhari Navaratri. Shakambhari is a goddess of nourishment. The story goes that she ended a drought by feeding every creature from her own form. Her temple in Saharanpur district, Uttar Pradesh, marks the day, and families hold a puja at home.

In 2027 the Navaratri runs from Pausha Shukla Ashtami, Saturday, 16 January, to Purnima. That's eight tithis in seven days. One tithi, Dwadashi, never touches a sunrise this time, the same short Dwadashi that gives [Paush Putrada Ekadashi two fast days](/blog/paush-putrada-ekadashi).

## Outside India

Abroad, the tithi falls at different local times, and in two big cities it moves to Thursday.

| City | Paush Purnima | Purnima runs (local time) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| London | Friday, 22 January | Thu 4:01 pm to Fri 12:17 pm |
| New York | Friday, 22 January | Thu 11:01 am to Fri 7:17 am |
| Toronto | Thursday, 21 January | Thu 11:01 am to Fri 7:17 am |
| San Francisco | Thursday, 21 January | Thu 8:01 am to Fri 4:17 am |
| Dubai | Friday, 22 January | Thu 8:01 pm to Fri 4:17 pm |
| Singapore | Friday, 22 January | Fri 12:01 am to 8:17 pm |
| Sydney | Friday, 22 January | Fri 3:01 am to 11:17 pm |

New York and Toronto share a clock but not a sunrise. In New York the sun rises at 7:14 am on Friday, three minutes before Purnima ends, so Friday keeps it. Toronto's sunrise is at 7:44 am, so there Purnima touches no sunrise at all, and our engine gives it Thursday. Check your temple's calendar too.

## Common questions

### Paush Purnima 2027 kab hai?

On Friday, 22 January 2027. Purnima runs from 9:31 pm on Thursday, 21 January, to 5:47 pm on Friday (IST).

### Is Paush Purnima 2027 a supermoon?

Yes. It's the closest full moon of 2027, 357,644 km from Earth at 5:47 pm IST on 22 January.

### When is Shakambhari Purnima 2027?

On Friday, 22 January, the same day as Paush Purnima. It ends Shakambhari Navaratri, which begins on Saturday, 16 January.

### When does kalpvas start in 2027?

On Paush Purnima, Friday, 22 January. It ends with the dip on Maghi Purnima, Saturday, 20 February.
