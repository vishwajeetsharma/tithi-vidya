**Dattatreya Jayanti 2026 is on Wednesday, 23 December, for most families.** It falls on Margashirsha Purnima, and the full moon tithi runs from 10:47 am on Wednesday to 6:58 am on Thursday, 24 December (IST).

So why do some calendars print Thursday? Because this Purnima is unusually short, and it ends just around sunrise. Whether it's still running at Thursday's sunrise depends on your city. And there's a reason it's so short: it's the closest full moon of 2026.

## Dattatreya Jayanti 2026 date and time

| | Date and time (IST) |
| --- | --- |
| Dattatreya (Datta) Jayanti | Wednesday, 23 December |
| Margashirsha Purnima begins | Wednesday, 23 December, 10:47 am |
| Purnima ends (the exact full moon) | Thursday, 24 December, 6:58 am |
| Next year | Monday, 13 December 2027 |

Datta Jayanti is [celebrated on the full moon day of Margashirsha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Datta_Jayanti). The tithi times are the same across India, because a tithi starts and ends at one moment everywhere. They come from Tithi Vidya, which works out the sun and moon from NASA JPL's [DE440 data](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/doc/de440_de441.html) ([here's how](/how-it-works)).

## Why some calendars say 24 December

Most Hindu calendars name a day after the tithi running at sunrise. It's called the udaya tithi rule, and [the Dev Uthani post shows it in action](/blog/dev-uthani-ekadashi). This Purnima ends at 6:58 am on Thursday. But Thursday's sunrise comes at different times in different cities:

| City | Sunrise, Thursday 24 December | Purnima at that sunrise? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New Delhi | 7:11 am | No, it ended at 6:58 am |
| Mumbai | 7:08 am | No |
| Pune | 7:03 am | No |
| Hyderabad | 6:43 am | Yes, for 15 minutes |
| Varanasi | 6:41 am | Yes |
| Bengaluru | 6:38 am | Yes |
| Patna | 6:33 am | Yes |
| Chennai | 6:28 am | Yes |
| Kolkata | 6:13 am | Yes, for 45 minutes |

So in Delhi, Mumbai and Pune, Purnima touches neither Wednesday's sunrise nor Thursday's. It's a skipped tithi (kshaya) there, and the calendar gives it to Wednesday. In the east and south, a sunrise calendar names Thursday "Purnima".

Wednesday, though, is the only day with Purnima in its daytime and its evening, everywhere in India: from 10:47 am until well after dark. That's why Wednesday, 23 December, is the date most calendars give for Datta Jayanti, including the government's [Incredible India festival page](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/festivals-and-events/maharashtra/shree-datta-jayanti). If your family or temple follows a calendar that says Thursday, go with it.

## Why this full moon is so short: a supermoon

A tithi usually lasts about 23½ hours, and [it can be as short as 19 hours 59 minutes or as long as 26 hours 47 minutes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tithi). This one lasts 20 hours 11 minutes, just 12 minutes above the shortest a tithi can be.

The reason is the moon's distance. Its orbit is an oval, and the moon moves fastest when it's closest to Earth, so it races through its tithis then. I worked it out from the same NASA data. The full moon at 6:58 am IST on 24 December is 356,738 km from Earth: the closest full moon of 2026. The moon reaches its nearest point of the month the same afternoon.

A full moon at its closest point is what [NASA calls a supermoon](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/supermoons/). So step outside on Wednesday evening: it's the biggest-looking full moon of the year.

## Who is Dattatreya?

Dattatreya is seen as a combined form of Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva, the son of the sage Atri and his wife Anasuya. The same Wikipedia page lists his most important places of worship in Karnataka, Maharashtra, Andhra Pradesh and Gujarat. Among them are Ganagapur, Pithapuram, Audumbar in Sangli district and Narasimha Wadi in Kolhapur district.

On the day, people bathe early in rivers and fast. Devotees also read the Guru Charitra, the book on the life of Narasimha Saraswati.

## Common questions

### Datta Jayanti kab hai in 2026?

On Wednesday, 23 December 2026, by most calendars. Purnima runs from 10:47 am that day to 6:58 am on Thursday (IST).

### Is Margashirsha Purnima on 23 or 24 December 2026?

Both appear. Purnima runs from 10:47 am on the 23rd to 6:58 am on the 24th. It touches no sunrise in Delhi, Mumbai or Pune, so calendars there give it to the 23rd. In the east and south, it's running at Thursday's sunrise, so sunrise calendars there say the 24th.

### Is the December 2026 full moon a supermoon?

Yes. By NASA JPL's data it's the closest full moon of 2026, 356,738 km from Earth, at 6:58 am IST on 24 December.

### When is Datta Jayanti in 2027?

On Monday, 13 December 2027. Purnima runs from 11:55 pm on Sunday, 12 December, to 9:38 pm on Monday (IST). So that year, Monday has it at sunrise and in the evening.
