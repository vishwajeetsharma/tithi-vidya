**Kaal Bhairav Jayanti 2026 is on Tuesday, 1 December.** The Ashtami tithi runs from 12:12 am to 11:14 pm that day (IST). So it covers Tuesday evening and the first hours of the night, when Bhairav is worshipped. The day is also called Kalabhairav Jayanti or Bhairav Ashtami.

That night matters more than you might think. Most festivals take the tithi running at sunrise, but Bhairav's day is picked by the night. In 2027, that rule moves it a whole day earlier than a sunrise calendar would show.

## Kaal Bhairav Jayanti 2026 date and time

| | Date and time (IST) |
| --- | --- |
| Kaal Bhairav Jayanti | Tuesday, 1 December |
| Ashtami begins | Tuesday, 1 December, 12:12 am |
| Ashtami ends | Tuesday, 1 December, 11:14 pm |
| Sunset in New Delhi | Tuesday, 5:24 pm |

It falls on Krishna Ashtami, the 8th day of the waning moon. In North India's calendar that's in the month of Margashirsha, and in the amanta calendar of the south and west it's still Kartika. It's the same day under two names, because the two calendars [start their months at different moons](/blog/tithi-birthday#the-three-parts-of-your-tithi-birthday).

## Why Kaal Bhairav Jayanti is kept at night

Kaal Bhairav is Shiva in his fierce form, and his puja has always belonged to the night. So the rule many panchangs follow, from the old text Vratraj, is about the night too. Keep the fast on the day when Ashtami is still running after the evening hours. If Ashtami ends before evening, the day before gets it.

In 2026 there's no doubt. Ashtami lasts until 11:14 pm on Tuesday, nearly 6 hours after sunset in Delhi. The years either side show the rule at work:

| Year | Ashtami runs (IST) | Kaal Bhairav Jayanti |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026 | 12:12 am to 11:14 pm, 1 Dec | Tuesday, 1 December |
| 2027 | 7:17 pm, 20 Nov, to 5:20 pm, 21 Nov | Saturday, 20 November |
| 2028 | 3:40 pm, 9 Nov, to 2:03 pm, 10 Nov | Thursday, 9 November |

Look at 2027. Ashtami is running at sunrise on Sunday, 21 November, so a plain sunrise calendar would print Sunday. But it ends at 5:20 pm, before Sunday's evening. The only night with Ashtami in it is Saturday's, so that's when the puja happens.

The dates come from Tithi Vidya, which works out the sun and moon from NASA JPL's [DE440 data](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/doc/de440_de441.html).

## The story of Kaal Bhairav

The story is told in the Puranas. Brahma and Vishnu were arguing over who was greater, and Shiva appeared between them as an endless pillar of light. Brahma claimed he had found its top, which was a lie. Shiva then took the form of Bhairav, who cut off Brahma's fifth head as punishment.

The skull stuck to Bhairav's hand, and the sin of killing a Brahmin followed him everywhere. Only when he reached Kashi did the skull fall away. That's why Varanasi calls him its kotwal, the city's guardian, and why a visit to Kashi isn't complete without his darshan. [Wikipedia's page on Bhairava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalabhairava) has the longer version.

His vahana, or mount, is a dog. "Kaal" means time, and "Bhairav" means the fearsome one: he's the lord of time, whom even death fears.

## How people keep Kaal Bhairav Jayanti

- **A fast** through the day, broken after the evening puja. If you're pregnant, diabetic or on medicine, talk to your doctor before a fast.
- **The night puja.** A lamp of mustard oil, black sesame and flowers, often with the Kalabhairava Ashtakam, the hymn by Adi Shankaracharya. Some families stay awake for a jagran.
- **Feeding dogs.** Rotis and milk for dogs are a common offering, since the dog is Bhairav's companion.
- **Temple visits,** above all to the Kaal Bhairav temples of Varanasi and Ujjain.

> **Did you know?** At the Kal Bhairav temple in Ujjain, liquor is offered to the deity. The custom comes from tantric forms of worship, where it's one of the traditional offerings.

## Common questions

### Kaal Bhairav Jayanti kab hai in 2026?

On Tuesday, 1 December 2026. The Ashtami tithi runs from 12:12 am to 11:14 pm that day, so the evening puja falls inside it.

### Why does Kaal Bhairav have a dog?

The dog is his vahana, the animal he rides. That's why feeding dogs is one of the most common offerings on Kalashtami and on his Jayanti.

### When is Kaal Bhairav Jayanti in 2027?

On Saturday, 20 November 2027. Ashtami begins at 7:17 pm that evening and ends before the next evening, so Saturday night is the one that counts.
