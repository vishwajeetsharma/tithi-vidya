**Saphala Ekadashi 2027 is on Sunday, 3 January, the first Ekadashi of the new year.** The Ekadashi tithi runs from 2:24 pm on Saturday, 2 January, to 4:08 pm on Sunday (IST). Break the fast on Monday, 4 January, between 7:15 am and 9:19 am in New Delhi.

In the US, though, it's a day earlier: Saturday, 2 January. And in San Francisco, the window to break the fast lasts just 10 minutes. Both are below.

Yes, 2027 has two of them. It has [25 Ekadashis instead of the usual 24](/blog/ekadashi-list), and Saphala Ekadashi opens and closes the year.

The tithi times are the same across India, because a tithi starts and ends at one moment everywhere. They come from Tithi Vidya, which works out the sun and moon from NASA JPL's [DE440 data](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/doc/de440_de441.html) ([here's how](/how-it-works)).

## Which month is it in?

Saphala Ekadashi is the Ekadashi of Krishna Paksha, the dark half of the moon's month. North Indian calendars put it in Pausha, as [Wikipedia's Ekadashi list does](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekadashi), with Narayana (Vishnu) as its deity. Calendars in the south and west, which start each month after the new moon, put the same Ekadashi in Margashirsha. It's the same day either way. Only the month's name differs.

## One day for families and Vaishnavas

Ekadashi fasts follow two sets of rules. Most families (Smarta) go by the tithi at sunrise. Vaishnava families, including ISKCON, also need Ekadashi to be running at dawn, about 96 minutes before sunrise.

In India, both rules point to Sunday this time. Ekadashi begins on Saturday afternoon, so it's running at Sunday's dawn and sunrise everywhere in the country. That isn't always so: [Dev Uthani Ekadashi in November](/blog/dev-uthani-ekadashi) split the two traditions across two days.

## In the US, it's Saturday, 2 January

In New York, Ekadashi runs from 3:54 am on Saturday to 5:38 am on Sunday. It's running at Saturday's sunrise but over before Sunday's, so the fast is on **Saturday, 2 January**, by both sets of rules. San Francisco is the same.

Breaking the fast is less simple there. Hari Vasara ends at 12:10 pm on Sunday, New York time, which is past the morning. Calendars avoid breaking the fast in the middle of the day, so the window moves to the afternoon:

San Francisco's window is only 10 minutes. Hari Vasara ends at 9:10 am there, and the morning period ends at 9:21 am. ISKCON temples publish their own times for each city, so if you follow one, use its calendar.

## The name, and the story

"Saphala" is the Sanskrit [saphala](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%AB%E0%A4%B2): successful, fruitful, bearing fruit. The name suits its story, which is all about fruit.

The story is told in the Bhavishya-uttara Purana, as Krishna's answer to King Yudhishthira. [ISKCON Mumbai's telling](https://www.iskconmumbai.com/blog/hare-krishna-blogs-1/glories-of-saphala-ekadasi-7) goes like this:

1. King Mahishmata ruled the city of Champavati. His eldest son, Lumpaka, lived badly, and the king exiled him to the forest.
2. Lumpaka lived under an old banyan tree, eating fallen fruit.
3. On the night before this Ekadashi, he couldn't sleep for the cold. Weak with hunger the next day, he gathered fruit and placed it at the foot of the tree, as an offering to Vishnu.
4. Without meaning to, he had fasted, stayed awake and offered fruit on Ekadashi. He changed his ways, and his father gave him back the kingdom.

That's why many people offer fresh fruit on this Ekadashi, and some stay awake through its night.

## Common questions

### Saphala Ekadashi kab hai in 2027?

On Sunday, 3 January 2027, in India. The tithi runs from 2:24 pm on Saturday to 4:08 pm on Sunday (IST). There's a second one on Thursday, 23 December 2027.

### Why is Saphala Ekadashi on 2 January in the US?

Because of the time difference. In New York, Ekadashi begins at 3:54 am on Saturday and ends at 5:38 am on Sunday, before sunrise. So Saturday is the only day that starts in Ekadashi there.

### When do I break the Saphala Ekadashi fast?

In India, on Monday morning, 4 January: 7:15 am to 9:19 am in New Delhi. Other cities are in the table above. In the US, it's on Sunday, 3 January.

### Is Saphala Ekadashi the same as Pausha Putrada Ekadashi?

No. Saphala is the dark-half Ekadashi. Pausha Putrada Ekadashi comes about two weeks later, in the bright half: Monday, 18 January 2027 (19 January for Vaishnavas).
