**Shattila Ekadashi 2027 is on Tuesday, 2 February.** The Ekadashi tithi runs from 8:42 am on Monday, 1 February, to 11:11 am on Tuesday (IST). Break the fast on Wednesday, 3 February, between 7:08 am and 9:19 am in New Delhi.

The name gives the theme away: *shat* means six, and *til* means sesame. An old text lists six ways to use til on this day, and they're all below. One more thing: in the US, Canada and the UK, the fast falls a day earlier, on Monday.

This time families and Vaishnavas keep the same day. Ekadashi is running at Tuesday's sunrise, and the next tithi, Dwadashi, lasts past Wednesday's, so there's nothing to split them. Compare [Paush Putrada Ekadashi](/blog/paush-putrada-ekadashi) two weeks earlier, which has two fast days.

The times come from Tithi Vidya, which works out the sun and moon from NASA JPL's [DE440 data](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/doc/de440_de441.html) ([here's how](/how-it-works)).

## The six ways to use til

The six come from the Padma Purana, in its chapter on [the vow of Shattila Ekadashi](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/the-padma-purana/d/doc365619.html) (Uttara Khanda, chapter 42). It places this Ekadashi in the dark half of Magha, and lists them like this:

1. **Bathe** in water with til in it (til snan).
2. **Rub** til on the body (til ubtan).
3. **Offer** til into the sacred fire (til havan).
4. **Offer water** with til, the way it's offered to ancestors (til tarpan).
5. **Give** til away (til daan).
6. **Eat** til (til bhojan).

The same chapter says black til is best for the bath and the food. It also stresses giving: til and clothes, as much as you can afford, to someone worthy of them.

> **Did you know?** Last year Shattila Ekadashi fell on 14 January 2026, the very day of Makar Sankranti, another til festival. This year it comes 19 days later, because 2026 had an extra month. [Makar Sankranti 2027](/blog/makar-sankranti) has its own puzzle this year: 14 or 15 January.

## The story of Shattila Ekadashi

In the Padma Purana's telling, Krishna tells the story to Yudhishthira. A Brahmin woman kept many fasts but never gave anything away. When Krishna came to her door as a beggar, she threw a lump of clay into his bowl.

After she died, she found a fine house waiting in heaven, with no grain in it. She learned the Shattila vow, kept it, and her house filled with grain and wealth. The point the story makes is that a fast without giving is only half a fast.

> **Tip:** If you're pregnant, diabetic or on medicine, talk to your doctor before a fast.

## Outside India: Monday in the West

In London and North America, the clock is hours behind India's. There, Ekadashi begins on Sunday evening or in the early hours of Monday, and it's over before Tuesday's sunrise. So Monday is the only day that starts in Ekadashi.

| City | Fast day | Break the fast |
| --- | --- | --- |
| London | Monday, 1 February | Tue 2 Feb, 1:10 pm to 3:01 pm |
| New York | Monday, 1 February | Tue 2 Feb, 7:21 am to 9:07 am |
| Toronto | Monday, 1 February | Tue 2 Feb, 7:33 am to 9:32 am |
| San Francisco | Monday, 1 February | Tue 2 Feb, 7:13 am to 9:17 am |
| Dubai | Tuesday, 2 February | Wed 3 Feb, 7:01 am to 9:14 am |
| Singapore | Tuesday, 2 February | Wed 3 Feb, 7:17 am to 9:41 am |
| Sydney | Tuesday, 2 February | Wed 3 Feb, 6:18 am to 9:02 am |

London's window is in the afternoon. The first quarter of Dwadashi, Hari Vasara, isn't a time to break a fast, and there it lasts until 12:21 pm. ISKCON temples follow their own rules abroad, so check your temple's calendar too.

## Common questions

### Shattila Ekadashi 2027 mein kab hai?

On Tuesday, 2 February 2027, in India. The Ekadashi tithi runs from 8:42 am on Monday to 11:11 am on Tuesday (IST).

### What is the parana time for Shattila Ekadashi 2027?

On Wednesday, 3 February: 7:08 am to 9:19 am in New Delhi, and 7:12 am to 9:28 am in Mumbai. The table above has 10 more cities.

### Can we eat til on Shattila Ekadashi?

Yes. Eating til is one of the six uses the Padma Purana lists for the day.

### When is the next Ekadashi after Shattila?

Jaya Ekadashi, on Wednesday, 17 February 2027. The [Ekadashi 2027 list](/blog/ekadashi-list) has all 25, with parana times.
