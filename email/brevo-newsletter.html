Why your Hindu birthday moves every year

Twelve moon months add up to about 354 days, 11 days short of a calendar year. So your tithi birthday comes earlier each year, until an extra month pulls it back. The article shows one tithi from 2026 to 2037.

Read the article →
 

Did you know?

A panchang day starts at sunrise, not midnight. Sunrise comes at different times in different cities, so now and then the same tithi lands on different dates in Delhi and Chennai.
