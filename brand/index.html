Brand guidelines

Moon days, made simple

Tithi Vidya helps anyone find their moon birthday, simply, privately and accurately. Calm, warm, trustworthy and modern, with a sense of wonder about the moon.

Logo

A moon at the start of its waxing half, Shukla Paksha: a bright crescent grows on the right of a blue-to-purple disc. Use the files as they are.

Clear space

Keep at least half the mark's height clear on every side. Smallest sizes: the mark at 16 px, the horizontal lockup at 120 px wide (30 mm in print), the stacked lockup at 80 px.

Do
  • Use the colour logo on light backgrounds.
  • Use the white logo on dark or gradient backgrounds.
  • Use the one-colour logo where the gradient can't print.
Don't
  • Change colours, stretch, rotate or add effects.
  • Change the moon's phase or put the crescent on the left.
  • Retype "Tithi Vidya" in another font.
  • Put the colour logo on photos or the brand gradient.

Typography

Display · Fraunces, soft, 600

Two birthdays

Headings, the wordmark and big numbers. Sentence case. One italic accent word per heading, in the text gradient.

Body · Plus Jakarta Sans

Find my tithi

Body text at 400, labels and buttons at 600 to 700. At least 16 px, about 70 characters a line.

Icons

24 px grid, 2 px round stroke, no fill, in the colour of the text around them. Shown here in indigo.

Voice

Like this

"Your tithi birthday falls on a different date each year. Here is the simple reason."

Plain English, short sentences, warm and honest about limits.

Not like this

"Unlock the cosmic secrets hidden in your stars."

No mystical language, hype or fortune-telling.
